How this report was made

  1. Reviews300 reviews from Google Play, newest first, dated 2026-06-02 to 2026-10-04. Taken from the store's public review page.
  2. Jev labels each reviewJev (jev-1.13.0), TypeSafe's model for typed judgments, read every review and answered narrow questions about it: how the reviewer feels, how severe any problem is, which of 18 product areas it concerns, and whether it's a bug, a feature request, a sign they'll leave, or tied to an update. Each answer comes with a probability. All of Jev's answers cost about $0.09.
  3. Code counts and ranksA label counts only when Jev is at least 60% sure; 40%–60% goes to the borderline list instead. Every count, ranking, and priority score is computed in code. Star ratings, dates, and versions are never shown to Jev.
  4. Quotes stay verbatimEvery quote is in the reviewer's own words; nothing is paraphrased or generated. Jev picks the sentence that makes the point, and when none stands out the review is shown as written, shortened if long. The summary below was written by Claude from these counts and quotes.

What the labels mean

Priority
How urgent an area is; areas are ranked by it. Each review reporting a problem there adds 1, plus 1× its severity (0–3), plus 2 if the reviewer says they'll leave, plus 1 if they tie it to an update.
Likely rank
Where the area could rank with a different sample of reviews: the range it fell in for 90% of 500 resamples of these reviews. The dot is its rank here. Areas whose ranges overlap aren't clearly ordered.
By month
Each bar is the share of that month's reviews (or week's, for short windows) reporting a problem in the area, on one scale for all areas. Faded bars: periods with fewer than 20 reviews.
Fewer / more lately
Complaints about the area fell (or rose) between the earlier and later half of the reviews by more than chance would usually explain (one-sided Fisher exact test, p < 0.025). A prompt to check releases or analytics, not proof.
Issues
Reviews reporting a problem in that area, and their share of all reviews. A review can count in several areas.
Severity
The average on a 0–3 scale: 1 minor, 2 degraded, 3 blocking.
Blocking
The app or the reviewer's main task doesn't work at all, or they lost data or money.
Degraded
Something works poorly or unreliably, or a feature is missing or was removed.
Minor
A cosmetic issue, small inconvenience, or dislike of a design or business decision.
Churn risk
Says they uninstalled, cancelled, or are switching, or will if nothing changes.
Since update
Ties the problem to a recent update, new version, or redesign.
Bug
Describes something in the app that is broken or behaves incorrectly.
Request
Asks for something to be added, or for a removed feature to come back.
Repro detail
Gives a detail an engineer could use to reproduce the problem: device, OS version, steps, or trigger.
Praise
Reviews praising that area.
Sentiment
How the reviewer feels, judged from their words, not their rating. Five levels, from very negative to very positive.
Unsure
Jev was 40%–60% sure. Unsure labels aren't counted anywhere; they're listed for a human to check.

Todoist on Google Play: what to fix first

Headline. Most of the 300 newest US reviews (2 Jun to 4 Oct 2026) are positive, but 102 report a problem and 26 are blocking. The biggest problem is getting into the app at all.

Fix first

The top three, in rough order. Click an area to read the reviews.

  1. Sign-up, login, and account: 23 reviews, 15 blocking, 5 churn. Google sign-in, password rules, captcha, and verification emails fail, so people can't get in.
  2. Design, redesign, and navigation: 20 reviews, 3 blocking, 6 churn (the most of any area). Reviewers want the old look and swipe behavior back as an option, not a rollback.
  3. Pricing and Pro paywall: 19 reviews, 3 blocking, 4 churn. The objection is core features (calendar view, more projects) being Pro-only.

Roughly tied after these: reminders that don't fire, stability, and recurring tasks that skip days.

Bugs worth an engineer's look
  • Captcha verification fails at login (v12254, v12250); the verification email never arrives (v12202).
  • Reminders don't fire at the set time (v12282, v12278, v12270).
  • The app is stuck on the welcome screen (v12172).
  • Recurring subtasks lose their order on the next recurrence (v12296).
  • The home-screen widget broke after an update on Oxygen OS 16 (v12174).

Feature requests and churn

26 reviews ask for something, each idea from fewer than 5 reviews: the old swipe behavior or a "legacy" dark theme, offline mode, a task start time, and subscription tiers. 15 reviews say they'll leave, mostly over design (6), login (5), pricing (4), and AI (3); a review can count in more than one.

What users love

Don't break design and ease of use (24 praise reviews), projects and labels (12), Ramble voice capture (11), or integrations such as calendar sync (11).

Caveats
  • The 300 newest US English reviews only; recent written reviews skew more critical than the 4.61★ store average.
  • 46 reviews have a borderline label that isn't counted; 9 are non-English.
  • Sharing, calendar views, sync, support, and devices each have fewer than 5 issue reviews, so treat them as anecdotes.
